Listening history (F47). Every play on this device is kept with its time, device, where it came from and whether it was skipped or completed. History shows them by day with All, Skipped and Completed, and a tap plays a song again. Settings has a switch to stop keeping plays and a Clear history action with an undo. The log stays on the device; core exports its query API (top tracks, artists and albums, plays per track, last played, skip rate) and a sync interface whose only target for now sends nothing.
