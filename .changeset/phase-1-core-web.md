Phase 1, core and web. Sign in to a Jellyfin server by address, user and password or with Quick Connect (F1, F2); the token is encrypted in the browser with a key the page cannot export (F5). Home with My songs, Recently played and Made for you in simple mode, plus Recently added and Most played in advanced (F7, F84). Library with artists, album artists, albums, songs, genres, years and playlists in grid and list, sorted and filtered from the URL (F8, F15), all from a local IndexedDB cache with a delta refresh (F14). Album, artist, playlist and favourites pages (F9, F10, F12, F13). Instant library search under the Ask bar with recent searches (F11, F75). Gapless playback through two media elements with direct play or transcoding by network class, a persisted queue with drag reorder, play next, add to end, clear, history, shuffle with an album-aware option, repeat, a now-playing screen, media keys through MediaSession, and play reporting so play counts work everywhere (F20 to F27). Settings with the simple or advanced switch, theme, language, streaming quality per network and sign-out. Every string in English and Gujarati. Mobile gains the mirrored TrackRow and AlbumCard components and the shared icon set; its screens follow in phase 3.

After the first round of review: an artist page shows every song and album whatever id the server holds for the name (Jellyfin keeps two entities for one artist when a library gains artist folders later), and works with the server down; a reload no longer counts as a play or starts a transcode; a token the server revoked sends you to sign-in with a sentence instead of a wall of errors; playlist reorders are read back from Jellyfin and compensated for 10.10's off-by-one, with the toast after the read-back and an undo; new playlists are private to you; every request times out (8 s on sign-in, 15 s elsewhere) with the "could not reach" sentence; the cache fetches what a refresh missed and keeps time by the server's clock; sign-out removes your library, queue and token from the browser; a scheme-less address tries https first and says so before using http; placeholder art matches the row, the bar fits a tablet, now playing fits a desktop and a phone, and genre, year and placeholder labels read in the light theme.
