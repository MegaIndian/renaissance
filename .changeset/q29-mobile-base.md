The phone app plays your library. Sign in by address, user and password or with Quick Connect; the sign-in token is kept in the phone's keychain (F1, F2, F5). Home in simple mode with the Ask bar's instant library results (F7, F11, F75, F84); the library with artists, albums, songs, genres and playlists, sortable and with a favourites filter, from a SQLite cache that works offline and refreshes from Jellyfin (F8, F14, F15); album, artist, genre, favourites and playlist pages, with hearts and playlist changes synced to Jellyfin and an undo on each (F9, F10, F12, F13). Now playing and the queue on react-native-track-player: the next song is buffered while this one plays, audio keeps going in the background, files the phone cannot play are converted by Jellyfin, and play counts reach the server (F20 to F24, F26, F27). English and Gujarati, light and dark. Core gains the library and session controllers the phone runs on, and an undo of a queue change keeps the current song playing. The web's settings wording now says "this device" where it said "this browser".
